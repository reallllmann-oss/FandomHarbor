# Product Vision

Fandom Harbor 是一个升级版 AO3 私域作品归档站。
它是邀请制进入的原创 / 同人作品归档平台。

目标不是做普通小说网站。
目标不是做论坛。
目标不是做社交平台。
目标不是复制 AO3 UI。

目标是：
1. 保留 AO3 的归档、标签、CP、分级、预警、评论、Kudos、收藏、推荐等核心体验。
2. 用现代化 UI 重做阅读体验。
3. 优化移动端。
4. 增加强后台管理。
5. 增加邀请码门禁。
6. 增加用户权限控制。
7. 增加反恶意举报机制。
8. 让站主更容易控制用户数据、内容审核和访问权限。

产品关键词：
- 私域
- 邀请制
- 作品归档
- 阅读体验优先
- 移动端友好
- 标签可治理
- 权限可控
- 反恶意举报
- 现代化 AO3

## Product principles

- Everything exists to improve reading, publishing, or project maintainability. Features that do none of these should be rejected.
- Archive first: stable discovery, metadata, preservation, and reading matter more than engagement loops.
- Reader comfort: typography, navigation, accessibility, and long-form reading are first-class concerns.
- Governable freedom: creators may use expressive free-form metadata while administrators can canonicalize it without erasing meaning.
- Least privilege: access is granted deliberately and enforced at every layer.
- Moderation with accountability: reports require evidence and a traceable process; defenses against malicious reporting are part of the design.
- Owner control: the site owner must be able to govern access, content, moderation, and data safely.

## Product identity

Fandom Harbor is an archive platform, reading platform, creator platform, and invitation-only community. Community features exist only where they support works and reading; the product is not a social network, forum, generic novel site, or blog.

## Success definition

- Readers can discover and read works comfortably on mobile and desktop.
- Authors can publish and maintain durable, well-described works through pen names.
- Administrators can govern access, tags, reports, content, and data with auditability.
- The system protects private identity and gated content by default.
- The architecture remains understandable, testable, reversible, and documented as the product grows.
