# Legacy Sprint 4 — Discover and Interact

Status: Superseded as a product-stage roadmap. Candidate work is now separated across Phase 4 — Archive and Phase 5 — Interaction.

Outcome: Readers find relevant works and use archive-centered interactions without social-feed behavior.

Envelope: series, ratings/warnings, typed/free-form/canonical tags, PostgreSQL search/filtering, bookmarks/recommendations, Kudos, approved comments and reading history.

Exit requires permission-safe search, tag governance, idempotent interactions, abuse controls, privacy decisions and responsive/accessibility acceptance.
