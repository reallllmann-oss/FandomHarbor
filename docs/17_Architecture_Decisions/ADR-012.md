# ADR-012 — Documentation-First Phase and Sprint Workflow

- Date: 2026-06-28
- Status: Accepted blueprint decision; implementation remains gated by Phase 0.6 approval
- Related .ai decisions: D-013, D-015

## Context

Large feature lists and code-first execution create hidden requirements and unreviewable scope.

## Decision

Use Product Phases for evolution and Sprints as bounded execution units. Research, requirements, architecture, database, API, UI, review, development, testing and documentation proceed in order.

## Consequences

Slower apparent start, lower rework and clearer approval. Phase templates and acceptance gates become mandatory.

## Alternatives rejected

- Leaving the concern implicit in implementation.
- Creating a parallel source of truth without documented ownership.
- Choosing short-term convenience over the project's security, archive and maintainability principles.

## Verification and review trigger

Review when its underlying product requirement changes, measured scale invalidates assumptions, a major provider/stack change is proposed, or an incident exposes a missing control.
