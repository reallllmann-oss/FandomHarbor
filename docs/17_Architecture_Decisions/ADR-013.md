# ADR-013 — Numbered Documentation and Decision Ownership

- Date: 2026-06-28
- Status: Accepted blueprint decision; implementation remains gated by Phase 0.6 approval
- Related .ai decisions: D-016, D-018

## Context

Unnumbered folders and mixed decision logs make sources difficult to find and maintain.

## Decision

Use the frozen numbered docs taxonomy. DECISIONS owns engineering/architecture memory, DESIGN_DECISIONS owns experience, ADRs hold permanent rationale, and apps/docs is read-only.

## Consequences

Creates strong discoverability and audit history; requires reference checks whenever files move or decisions change.

## Alternatives rejected

- Leaving the concern implicit in implementation.
- Creating a parallel source of truth without documented ownership.
- Choosing short-term convenience over the project's security, archive and maintainability principles.

## Verification and review trigger

Review when its underlying product requirement changes, measured scale invalidates assumptions, a major provider/stack change is proposed, or an incident exposes a missing control.
