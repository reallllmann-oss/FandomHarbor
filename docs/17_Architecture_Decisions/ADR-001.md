# ADR-001 — Why Supabase and PostgreSQL

- Date: 2026-06-28
- Status: Accepted blueprint decision; implementation remains gated by Phase 0.6 approval
- Related .ai decisions: D-008

## Context

Fandom Harbor needs authentication, relational constraints, private storage, database-level authorization and an owner-manageable platform without operating many services.

## Decision

Use Supabase for Auth/Storage/platform services and PostgreSQL as the relational source of truth with migrations and RLS.

## Consequences

Accelerates a coherent private archive; increases platform dependency, mitigated by portable PostgreSQL migrations, exports and restore drills.

## Alternatives rejected

- Leaving the concern implicit in implementation.
- Creating a parallel source of truth without documented ownership.
- Choosing short-term convenience over the project's security, archive and maintainability principles.

## Verification and review trigger

Review when its underlying product requirement changes, measured scale invalidates assumptions, a major provider/stack change is proposed, or an incident exposes a missing control.
