# ADR-007 — Modular Monolith and Purpose-Specific Packages

- Date: 2026-06-28
- Status: Accepted blueprint decision; implementation remains gated by Phase 0.6 approval
- Related .ai decisions: D-009, D-010

## Context

Premature services increase operational burden, while app-local duplication causes drift.

## Decision

Start as a modular monolith over one Supabase domain. Apps depend on explicit ui/editor/auth/database/services/types/constants/config/utils packages; no apps/shared.

## Consequences

Keeps deployment and transactions simple while enforcing dependency direction. Package boundaries must not become dumping grounds.

## Alternatives rejected

- Leaving the concern implicit in implementation.
- Creating a parallel source of truth without documented ownership.
- Choosing short-term convenience over the project's security, archive and maintainability principles.

## Verification and review trigger

Review when its underlying product requirement changes, measured scale invalidates assumptions, a major provider/stack change is proposed, or an incident exposes a missing control.
