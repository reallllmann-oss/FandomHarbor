# ADR-005 — AO3 Product Philosophy Without AO3 UI

- Date: 2026-06-28
- Status: Accepted blueprint decision; implementation remains gated by Phase 0.6 approval
- Related .ai decisions: D-001, D-004; DD-001–DD-004

## Context

AO3 demonstrates powerful archive metadata and creator control, but its visual design and mobile experience are not the product target.

## Decision

Inherit archive-first metadata, warnings, relationships and discoverability while building a modern reading-first, mobile-first design and refusing social drift.

## Consequences

Requires rich metadata UI without copying layouts; visual decisions live in DESIGN_DECISIONS and the Design System.

## Alternatives rejected

- Leaving the concern implicit in implementation.
- Creating a parallel source of truth without documented ownership.
- Choosing short-term convenience over the project's security, archive and maintainability principles.

## Verification and review trigger

Review when its underlying product requirement changes, measured scale invalidates assumptions, a major provider/stack change is proposed, or an incident exposes a missing control.
