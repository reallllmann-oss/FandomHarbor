# ADR-003 — Why Invitations Form a Trust Chain

- Date: 2026-06-28
- Status: Accepted blueprint decision; implementation remains gated by Phase 0.6 approval
- Related .ai decisions: D-003, D-005

## Context

Admission is private and abuse accountability matters. A one-time gate key alone cannot explain provenance or support responsible revocation.

## Decision

Model Invitations and redemptions with inviter, limits, expiry, revocation and ancestry. Admission never grants elevated roles.

## Consequences

Supports investigation and governance; lineage must not become automatic collective punishment and retention needs privacy limits.

## Alternatives rejected

- Leaving the concern implicit in implementation.
- Creating a parallel source of truth without documented ownership.
- Choosing short-term convenience over the project's security, archive and maintainability principles.

## Verification and review trigger

Review when its underlying product requirement changes, measured scale invalidates assumptions, a major provider/stack change is proposed, or an incident exposes a missing control.
