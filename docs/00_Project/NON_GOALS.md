# Product Non-Goals

Status: Proposed for Phase 0.6 approval

These are intentional refusals, not missing backlog items. Adding one requires explicit product-owner approval, a new decision that supersedes this scope and a review of product identity.

## Social and communication

- No private messaging or direct-message inbox.
- No chat rooms, group chats or live presence spaces.
- No social feed, activity stream or follower timeline.
- No follow-author/fan relationship graph.
- No repost/retweet/share-to-feed mechanic.
- No fan ranking, creator leaderboard or public competitive popularity score.
- No infinite-scroll social experience designed to maximize session time.

## Media and monetization

- No live streaming, live audio rooms or video platform.
- No advertising network, behavioral ad targeting or sponsored ranking.
- No creator marketplace, tipping economy or subscription commerce in the current blueprint.
- No DRM platform or commercial rights-management system.

## Discovery and automation

- No algorithm-driven timeline.
- No opaque personalized ranking that replaces explicit search/filter/archive navigation.
- No AI-generated content feature or AI search by default; `ai_search` remains disabled.
- No public search-engine indexing of gated work bodies.

## Community scope

- No generic forum unrelated to works and archive governance.
- No standalone blogging or personal-homepage platform.
- No gamified points, streaks, badges or engagement quests.
- No automatic punishment based solely on report count, dislikes or popularity.
- No public registration that bypasses invitation trust.

## Architectural non-goals

- No premature microservices.
- No duplicate editable documentation store in `apps/docs`.
- No client-only authorization.
- No production schema changes outside reviewed migrations.
- No single generic shared/services package used as a dumping ground.

Archive-centered comments, Kudos, bookmarks and recommendations are allowed because they support works and reading; they must not become a route back to social-feed mechanics.
