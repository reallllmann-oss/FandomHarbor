# Fandom Harbor Vision

Status: Proposed for Phase 0.6 approval

## Why Fandom Harbor exists

Long-form original and fan works deserve a durable home that respects creators, lets readers find precisely what they want, and remains comfortable on modern devices. Existing archives prove the value of rich metadata and community stewardship, but private communities also need stronger access control, moderation accountability, identity separation and owner-governed data.

Fandom Harbor exists to make the archive itself trustworthy: works remain readable and recoverable; metadata remains expressive but governable; public pen names remain separate from private accounts; invitations carry responsibility; and moderation actions are traceable without turning the platform into surveillance.

## Problems it solves

- Rich archives can be visually dense and uncomfortable on mobile or during long reading sessions.
- Generic publishing platforms flatten fandom-specific ratings, warnings, relationships and tags.
- Social platforms optimize timelines and popularity rather than preservation and intentional discovery.
- Private communities need invitation lineage, explicit roles, RLS, auditability and anti-abuse processes.
- Authors need durable drafts, versions, Diff and restore under multiple pen names.
- Site owners need coherent documentation and maintainable architecture instead of fragile one-off features.

## Five-year direction

In five years, Fandom Harbor should be a dependable private archive platform that a trusted community can operate without rebuilding its foundations. It should support excellent multilingual long-form reading, durable creator publishing, mature tag governance, accountable moderation, privacy-conscious insights and tested data recovery.

Growth is measured by archive health and trust—not by virality. The platform may serve a larger invitation network, but it must remain governable, comprehensible and portable enough that owners are not trapped by opaque systems.

## What success means

- Readers can consistently discover and comfortably finish works on mobile and desktop.
- Authors can publish, revise and restore works without losing attribution or history.
- Tags, ratings, warnings and relationships improve discovery without erasing author intent.
- Invitations, permissions, reports and privileged operations are secure and auditable.
- Private identity and behavioral data are minimized and protected.
- Backups restore successfully and migrations remain safe.
- New contributors can understand the architecture and make changes without creating parallel truths.
- The product resists social drift, advertising pressure and engagement manipulation.

## Vision guardrail

Fandom Harbor does not aim to be everything a community could do online. It aims to be unusually good at archiving, publishing, discovering and reading works inside a trusted private environment.
