# Product Risk Register

Status: Proposed for Phase 0.6 approval

| ID    | Product risk                                                              | Impact                                        | Treatment                                                                   |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PR-01 | Social drift turns archive interactions into a feed/fan platform          | Product identity and reading quality collapse | Enforce NON_GOALS, Feature Flags and ADR-005                                |
| PR-02 | Scope includes every format/feature before text archive works             | Product never ships a reliable core           | Text-first Phase sequence; KI-003 decision                                  |
| PR-03 | Invitation lineage becomes collective punishment                          | Trust system becomes abusive                  | Human review, no automatic descendant sanctions; resolve KI-014             |
| PR-04 | Anonymous comments promise privacy without clear moderator accountability | User expectations and safety conflict         | Resolve KI-002 before Phase 5                                               |
| PR-05 | Reports become a weapon against authors                                   | Chilling effect and moderation overload       | Evidence, history, limits, human decisions and appeal policy                |
| PR-06 | Analytics and Reading History violate private-community expectations      | Trust and legal/privacy risk                  | Data minimization, opt/control, retention decisions KI-008/KI-015           |
| PR-07 | Tag canonicalization erases author language or context                    | Archive quality and creator trust decline     | Preserve submitted labels/history/redirects; governed merges                |
| PR-08 | Ratings/warnings or age policy are unclear                                | Reader harm and launch/legal blockers         | Resolve KI-004/KI-012 before relevant release                               |
| PR-09 | Admin power lacks staffing/process maturity                               | Slow or inconsistent moderation               | Least privilege, audit, workflow ownership and operational rehearsal        |
| PR-10 | Recommendations or metrics create hidden popularity ranking               | No Social Drift is undermined                 | Explicit archive-centered semantics; no public competitive leaderboards     |
| PR-11 | Documentation volume becomes ritual rather than useful truth              | Decisions drift despite many files            | Ownership registries, link checks, concise updates and retrospective review |

Product risks are reviewed at Phase planning/exit. A high-impact unresolved risk blocks the affected Phase, not necessarily unrelated work.
